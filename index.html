About the studio

Where simple yarn becomes meaningful handmade beauty.

Bloom & Loop was founded with the vision of transforming simple yarn into meaningful handmade creations that bring joy, comfort, and beauty to everyday life.

Founded by Asmita Lokande.S, the brand celebrates elegant crochet products shaped by patience, creativity, warm color palettes, and a love for handcrafted details.
